Privacy Policy

App Name: Tables Master

Package Name: com.rajkumarbaria.tablesmaster

Developer: Rajkumar Baria

Effective Date: October 7, 2026

1. No Personal Data Collection

Tables Master does not collect, track, transmit, or sell any personally identifiable information (PII).

2. Local Offline-First Storage

All student profiles, quiz scores, accuracy statistics, and mistakes are stored 100% locally on the device using an internal SQLite/Room database.

3. Google Drive Cloud Backup (Optional)

Cloud backup is entirely optional and initiated manually by the user. Data is saved directly into the user's private Google Drive storage. No proprietary external servers are used.

4. Permissions

  • VIBRATE: Used exclusively to provide haptic feedback when answering math questions.
  • INTERNET: Used strictly for optional user-initiated Google Drive backup and developer contact links.
  • Text-to-Speech (TTS): Uses Android's built-in TextToSpeech engine locally. No voice audio is recorded or stored.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliant)

Safe for children and students of all ages. No third-party advertisements, no profiling, and no analytics tracking.

6. Data Deletion

Users can clear mistake history, delete student profiles, or uninstall the app to permanently erase all data.
